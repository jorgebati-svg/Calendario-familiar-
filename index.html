Recommended

Recommended — preheat on the lowest rack for 30–45 min. It evens out hot spots in most home ovens and firms up the bottom crust.

Baking more than one pan at once? Rotate and swap rack positions halfway through, and add about 3–5 minutes.
Tip: focaccia dough is very wet, use a stand mixer or the stretch-and-fold technique, and generously oil your pan and hands instead of adding flour.
